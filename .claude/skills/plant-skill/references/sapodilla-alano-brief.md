Manilkara zapota (L.) P.Royen 'Alano' — sapodilla, chico, chiku, naseberry (Sapotaceae): the compact, heavy-bearing Florida cultivar, grafted onto a seedling rootstock. A slow, long-lived evergreen tree with milky white latex (chicle) in every part; its architecture is Aubréville's model: tiers of near-horizontal branches that grow on in short units, each unit ending in a tight rosette of glossy leaves, the next springing from just below it, so the crown is layered, dense and dark.
Seed and seedling: seed glossy black-brown, hard, flattened long-oval, 2 cm × 1 cm × 0.5 cm, a narrow white scar (hilum) 1.2 cm long down one edge and a small curved hook at one end. Germination epigeal after a month: a pale green hypocotyl hook 4 cm, then two thin leafy green seed leaves 2 × 1 cm lifting the black seed coat; then single alternate leaves and a first small rosette at the tip. A seedling is very slow: 30–40 cm at a year, its stem 5 mm thick, green turning brown.
Trunk and bark: the grafted tree a single short trunk; at 8 years 4.5 m tall, the trunk 13 cm thick, a crown 5 m wide, wider than tall, branched from 0.6 m. Young twigs green-brown with rusty-brown down at the tip and crowded leaf scars below each rosette; young bark light brown and finely striated; old bark dark brown (#4a3a2e), rough, deeply furrowed into small rectangular blocks, the furrows near black; every cut bleeds white latex beads.
Leaves: only in rosettes of 10–20 at the shoot tips (spiral, the internodes a few mm), none along the bare stems behind them. Blade elliptic, pointed at both ends, often a tiny notch at the tip, 9 × 3.5 cm (6–13 cm), leathery, margin entire and slightly wavy; upper side very glossy dark green with a pale midrib; underside paler matte grey-green, the midrib raised and pale yellow-green, fine parallel side veins at a wide angle. Petiole 2 cm, green. New flush leaves pinkish bronze, then light green; old leaves yellow before falling.
Flower: singly in the leaf axils within and just below the rosettes, hanging on a slender rusty-hairy stalk 1.5 cm; a small bell 1 cm across and 1 cm long: 6 sepals in two whorls of 3, rusty-brown and felted outside, the inner three paler; a cream-white corolla of 6 short lobes; 6 stamens and 6 small petal-like staminodes inside; a slender style poking out of the mouth.
Fruit: hangs singly or two or three together just below the rosettes, on a stalk 2 cm long, 3–4 mm thick. Fruitlet round, scurfy rusty-brown over green, the style a fine point at its tip; round until about 110 days, then a broad ovoid slightly pointed at the tip; mature at about 200 days, 7.7 cm long × 6.7 cm across, 180 g; 'Alano' skin smooth, light tan-brown with little scurf (thin sandy-brown scurf on unripe fruit). Cut: flesh honey-tan, translucent and darker round the centre, very fine like a pear, with 3–5 glossy black seeds in slots radiating from the core like spokes, a pale core.
Roots: a taproot and many laterals, most in the top 75 cm, light brown.
