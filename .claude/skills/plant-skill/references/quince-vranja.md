# Cydonia oblonga Mill. 'Vranja' (Serbian pear-shaped quince): facts for a procedural model

Researched 2026-11-05 for a quince tree grown stage by stage, from the seed or from a grafted nursery tree, to an old
tree in a Balkan garden or orchard. Built on the template `datasheet.md` and set out like `plum-hauszwetschge.md` and
`peach-saturn.md`.

**Labels used below**

- **[CV]**: measured or scored on 'Vranja' itself, under any of its names: 'Vranja Nenadović' (the RHS name),
  'Vranjska' (the Serbian papers), 'Riesenquitte von Vranja', 'Monstrueux de Vranja', 'Gigante de Vranja'. Values
  measured on fruit sold as **'Bereczki'** are marked **[CV-B]**: the name is treated as a synonym by GRIN and by
  the RHS, but Serbian trials and the UPOV guideline treat 'Bereczki' and 'Vranja' as two cultivars (section 0).
- **[SP]**: said of the species *Cydonia oblonga*, of quince in general, or of another quince cultivar.
- **[EST]**: my estimate for modelling, with the reasoning given. It is not a published value.
- **[2nd]**: read only in an abstract, a search snippet, a review citing it, a photo caption or a nursery claim, not
  in the measured original.

Each value has a source tag such as S1; the references are at the end. Units: millimetres for small organs,
centimetres for leaves and trunks, metres for trees, grams, days, years. "DAFB" means days after full bloom. A
grafted tree's age runs from planting a one-year nursery tree; a seedling's from sowing.

## 0. The cultivar in one paragraph

- **What it is.** An old, large-fruited, pear-shaped quince from the south of Serbia, named after the town of Vranje
  (S7 [2nd], S22 [2nd]). The RHS lists it as 'Vranja' Nenadović (S14). In Serbia it is the second cultivar after
  'Leskovačka' and is grown as its polleniser. "'Leskovacka' is characterized by high quality of fruits, but
  relatively low yield. Contrary, 'Vranjska' is characterized by higher yields, but lower fruit quality" (S3).
- **Its marks.** A **strong, upright** tree (UPOV vigour note 7, habit note 1: 'Vranja' is the example variety for
  both, S1) with **long leaves** (note 7), **large stipules** (note 7), **large dark pink flowers** with overlapping
  petals (notes 7, 3, 3) and **large pyriform fruit with a neck** (notes 7, 5, 9) (S1). Its leaf buds burst
  **early**, its flowering begins at a **medium** time (S1). It is **self-compatible** (S3).
- **The Bereczki question (a conflict).** GRIN calls 'Vranja' "a Serbian cultivar, possibly the same as Bereczki"
  and lists 'Vranja' among the names of its 'Bereczki' accession (S7 [2nd]). The RHS files the 'Vranja' of the UK
  trade as **'Vranja' misapplied = 'Bereczki'** (S14). But the Belgrade trials grew 'Vranjska' and 'Bereczki' side
  by side as different cultivars (S5 [2nd]), and the UPOV guideline uses both as example varieties with **different**
  states: leaf bud burst early ('Vranja') against medium ('Bereczki'), and stipules large ('Vranja') against "absent or
  very small" ('Bereczki') in the working draft (S1, S2). By SSR markers, 'Bereczki' falls in the 'Portugal' group
  (S8, S26 [2nd]). **Rule for the model:** build the Serbian 'Vranja' of S1 and S3. Use the 'Bereczki' fruit numbers
  only as a nearby check, tagged [CV-B].
- **Rules for the model.** One clonal, grafted tree on a quince rootstock (Quince A / MA or BA29). Flowers are single,
  at the tips of short leafy shoots of the current season. Fruit is a felted, pear-shaped, golden pome with a neck
  and a persistent calyx at the apex.

| Why this one | the second quince of Serbia, Europe's leading quince grower, as the polleniser of 'Leskovačka'; self-compatible, so one tree fruits; the large pear-shaped quince of the British and French trade (S3, S20, S14) |
| Food-forest layer | low tree layer (small tree, 3–5 m), under walnuts and tall pears or as the canopy of a small garden |
| Architecture (Hallé–Oldeman model) | **Scarrone's model [EST].** The trunk and main limbs grow orthotropically and rhythmically, one flush a year from a terminal bud. The fruiting shoots are short, current-season, leafy shoots that **end in one flower**, so each fruiting axis stops and goes on from a lateral bud below the flower: a sympodium. The short fruiting shoots are 10–15 cm long with 5–8 nodes, sympodial and "end the primary growth after flowering"; vegetative shoots are monopodial and reach 100 cm or more (S11). No source names a model for quince. Costes et al. put the apple, which also flowers terminally from mixed buds, in **Rauh's model** (S13). Scarrone's fits the terminal flowering on the branches better; Rauh's is the reading if only the monopodial young tree counts |

## 9. Gaps

Every row that is an estimate, and how it was made:

- **Architecture.** No source names a Hallé–Oldeman model for quince. Scarrone's is chosen from the terminal
  flowering on short sympodial shoots (S11); Costes et al. call the apple Rauh's (S13).
- **Phyllotaxis 2/5**: taken from apple and pear; no quince count found.
- **Germination type (epigeal)**, hypocotyl and cotyledon sizes, first-year seedling: from apple and pear seedlings.
  Seed width and thickness: scaled from the measured length (S24b).
- **Seeds per fruit for 'Vranja'** (20–30): the middle of the species range (S24a), larger fruit holding more.
- **Height, trunk and crown by age**: interpolated between the planting size (S23), the measured TCSA of ~5-year
  quinces (S25b) and the mature 3–5 m of 'Vranja' (S14, S15). No trunk was measured on 'Vranja'.
- **Leaf blade 8–11 × 5–7 cm**: the top of the species range (S9, S18), because UPOV puts 'Vranja' in the "long"
  class. No millimetre value was found for 'Vranja'.
- **Number of lateral veins**, sepal length in fruit, bark plate size and patch colours: from photographs and general
  quince descriptions.
- **Flowering and harvest dates in Serbia**: inferred from UPOV's "medium" flowering (S1), mid-May at Čačak (S20),
  late April in Turkey (S12) and GRIN's "October–November" ripening (S7). DAFB to harvest is from 'Eşme' (S12).
- **Shape index 1.15–1.30**: from the photo-caption sizes (S21); the Belgrade table row for 'Vranjska' (S5) could not be
  read, and its snippets disagree whether 'Vranjska' is grouped with the round or the elongated fruit.
- **Yield per tree** by age: from national and other-cultivar figures (S5, S25); the two Belgrade trials disagree for
  'Vranjska' (S4, S5).
- **Fruit colours** are 'Eşme''s measured CIELab (S12), not 'Vranja''s.
- **Leaf life** in days: from the Kyiv leaf-fall dates (S11) and early bud break (S1).
- **Roots and suckers**: general quince rootstock behaviour.

**Conflicts between sources**

- 'Vranja' = 'Bereczki'? GRIN (S7) and the RHS (S14) say yes or maybe; the Belgrade trials (S5), the UPOV guideline
  (S1, S2) and the SSR grouping of 'Bereczki' with 'Portugal' (S8, S26) say they differ.
- Fruit mass: 300–550 g (S21), 500–600 g (S16), "more than 1 kg" and "nearly 3 lb" (S23); measured 'Bereczki' 247 g
  (S6) and 283 g (S5).
- Tree height: 2.5–4 m (RHS table) and "up to 5 m" (RHS text) (S14); 3–5 m (S15); on Quince A 2.5–3 m to 4.5 m (S23).
- Stamens: "less than 1/2 as long as petals" (S9) against "equal to or slightly longer than petals" (S10).
- Stipules: "caducous, ovate" without glands (S9) against "glandular-serrate" (S10, S11).
- Flower colour of 'Vranja': dark pink (UPOV final, S1), pink (draft, S2), pink-tinged (S14), white-pink (S15, S16).
- Yield: high (S5, 2003–2004) against no better than the low-yielding standard (S4, 2010–2013).
- Bark: smooth grey-brown (S15, other cultivars), dark brown ('Vranja', S15), rectangular plates (S10), peeling to
  orange (S18).
- Fruit shape: pyriform with a neck (S1, S7, S14, S16, S21) against a snippet of S5 grouping 'Vranjska' with the
  round, apple-shaped fruit (unreliable, [2nd]).
- Bereczki ripening "at the end of September" (US nursery, S23) against 'Vranja' October–November (S7, S16).

## 10. For the reference sheet: what the eye sees

Hex values are estimates for a neutral daylight plate, except the fruit greens and yellows taken from S12.

- **Tree**: a small, upright, dense, rounded tree 3–5 m, often several-stemmed or leaning, its twigs crooked and
  zig-zag, the fruit hanging at the very tips of short shoots among the last leaves.
- **Trunk**: grey-brown (≈ `#6e655a`) with flaking plates, patches of orange-brown (≈ `#b0703a`), tan (≈ `#b59a74`)
  and grey-green (≈ `#8a8c74`) where plates have fallen.
- **One-year shoots**: purplish red to purple-brown (≈ `#6a3a34`), grey-felted near the tip (≈ `#b8b0a6`).
- **Leaf, upper face**: dark green, dull (≈ `#3f5a2e`). **Under face**: grey-white felt (≈ `#c8cbbd`), veins raised.
  **Unfolding leaves**: silvery white with hairs (≈ `#dcdcd0`), green stipules. **Autumn**: clear yellow (≈ `#d9b630`),
  then brown.
- **Flower**: 4–5 cm, a shallow cup of 5 overlapping petals, **pink in bud** (≈ `#d97a98`), open **pale pink**
  (≈ `#f2c6d2`) fading to near white (≈ `#f8ecef`) at the centre; 20 stamens with yellow anthers (≈ `#e8c040`) and
  white-to-purple filaments; 5 pale styles, woolly at the base; green, felted, reflexed sepals with gland teeth.
- **Fruitlet**: a small grey-white felted club (≈ `#c9cbb8`) with the green sepals spread at its top.
- **Summer fruit**: green (≈ `#89a043`) under thinning grey felt, pear-shaped with a neck.
- **Ripe fruit**: yellow-green to **golden** (≈ `#d1c756` → `#ddc137`), felt rubbed off to a smooth, faintly
  greasy skin with felt left in the hollows; the shoulder and five calyx ribs lumpy; brown, dry, reflexed sepals in
  the eye; a stalk 5 mm or none.
- **Flesh**: pale yellow (≈ `#f0e2a8`), browning fast when cut (≈ `#c89a5a`); five locules in a star, each with a
  stack of **brown seeds** (≈ `#5a3420`) in white mucilage.
