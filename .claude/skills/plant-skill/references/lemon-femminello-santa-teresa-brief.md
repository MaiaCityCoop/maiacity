Citrus × limon (L.) Osbeck 'Femminello Santa Teresa' (Femminello group) — the Sicilian lemon of the Ionian coast, budded on sour orange (Citrus × aurantium L.), an evergreen tree kept at 3.5–4 m in an orchard. Remontant: it flowers and sets fruit several times a year, so a grown tree carries ripe yellow lemons, green ones and white flowers at once.
Planted as a grafted nursery tree: a single straight stem 1–1.2 m high, 1.5–2 cm thick, the bud union 15–25 cm above the soil (a small step, the sour-orange stock below a little narrower and greener-grey), headed at 70–80 cm with three or four young shoots; a ball of fibrous yellow-brown roots.
Trunk and bark: a short trunk, 60–80 cm to the first limbs, 15–18 cm thick at 8 years; 3–4 main limbs rising at 40–60° into a dense rounded-oval crown, broader than tall, down to the ground on the outside. New shoots green and sharply angled (triangular in section), a stiff thorn 0.5–1.5 cm in some leaf axils (few and short on this cultivar, longer on water sprouts). Young bark smooth green-grey; old bark grey to grey-brown, fairly smooth with fine shallow longitudinal fissures, pale lichen spots.
Leaves: alternate, simple, evergreen, living about two years; blade 8–12 cm × 4–6 cm, elliptic-ovate, tip acute to slightly pointed, base wedge-shaped to rounded, margin finely crenate-serrate; upper side glossy dark green, under side paler yellow-green and matt, translucent oil dots against the light; petiole 1–1.5 cm with a very narrow wing, jointed at the blade. A new flush (spring, summer, autumn) comes out reddish-purple to bronze, soft and limp, greening in 3–4 weeks.
Flowers: single or in small clusters of 2–10 at the tips and in the axils of new shoots; buds plump, 1–1.5 cm, deep purple-red outside; open flower 3–4 cm across, four or five thick waxy petals 1.5–2 cm long, white inside, purple-flushed outside, curled back; 20–40 stamens with yellow anthers joined in bundles; a green stout style with a large yellow-green stigma; a small green five-lobed cup (calyx). Strongly scented.
Fruit: elliptic-oblong, 8–9 cm long × 5.5–6.5 cm across, about 120–150 g, a pronounced pointed nipple at the tip and a small neck or rounded shoulder at the stalk end, the persistent green calyx button at the stalk; skin finely pitted with oil glands, medium thick (5–7 mm), glossy; green and hard when young, full size dark green, then pale green-yellow, then bright lemon-yellow when ripe. Cut across: 8–10 segments of pale yellow juicy pulp round a small pithy core, white albedo inside the yellow flavedo, few or no seeds (0–5), seeds pale cream, ovoid, pointed, 9–10 mm.
Roots: the sour-orange stock's roots, a short taproot and many woody laterals spreading to the edge of the crown and beyond, most in the top 60 cm, fine fibrous feeder roots yellowish-brown.
