Citrus × limon (L.) Osbeck 'Femminello Santa Teresa' (Femminello group) — the Sicilian lemon of the Ionian coast, budded on sour orange (Citrus × aurantium L.), an evergreen tree kept at 3–3.6 m by pruning in an orchard (5 × 5 m). Remontant: it flowers and sets fruit several times a year, so a grown tree carries ripe yellow lemons, green ones and white flowers at once.
Planted as a two-year grafted nursery tree: a single straight stem about 1–1.1 m high, 1.2–1.8 cm thick, the bud union 20–30 cm above the soil (a small step, the sour-orange stock below a little narrower and greener-grey), headed at 70–80 cm with three or four young shoots; a ball of fibrous yellow-brown roots.
Trunk and bark: a short trunk, 50–70 cm to the first limbs, about 10 cm thick at 8 years and 12–16 cm at 10–12 years; 3–4 main limbs rising at 40–60° into a dense rounded globe of a crown, 3–3.5 m across at 8–12 years, leafy down to near the ground on the outside. New shoots green, sharply angled, flushed purple-red when young; nearly thornless: only now and then a short straight thorn 5–12 mm in a leaf axil of a vigorous shoot, none on fruiting twigs. Young bark smooth green-grey; old bark grey to grey-brown, fairly smooth with fine shallow longitudinal fissures, pale lichen spots.
Leaves: alternate, simple, evergreen, living about two years; blade 9–11 cm × 4.5–5.5 cm, elliptic-ovate, tip acute to slightly pointed, base wedge-shaped to rounded, margin finely crenate-serrate; upper side glossy dark green, under side paler yellow-green and matt, translucent oil dots against the light; petiole 1–1.5 cm with a very narrow wing, jointed at the blade. A new flush (spring, summer, autumn) comes out reddish-purple to bronze, soft and limp, greening in 3–4 weeks.
Flowers: single or in small clusters of 2–10 at the tips and in the axils of new shoots; buds plump, 1–1.5 cm, deep purple-red outside; open flower 3–4 cm across, four or five thick waxy petals 1.5–2 cm long, white inside, purple-flushed outside, curled back; 30–40 stamens with yellow anthers joined in bundles; a green stout style with a large yellow-green stigma; a small green five-lobed cup (calyx). Strongly scented.
Fruit: short-oval, the roundest of lemons: 6.7–7.5 cm long (with its nipple) × 6–6.4 cm across, about 120 g, a short blunt nipple only 7 mm high at the tip with a small round style scar, the base rounded (hardly a neck), the persistent green star-shaped calyx button (8–10 mm) on a short woody stalk 5–15 mm; skin finely pitted with oil glands, 4–6 mm thick, glossy; green and hard when young, full size dark green, then pale green-yellow, then bright lemon-yellow when ripe. Cut across: about 10 segments of pale yellow juicy pulp round a small solid pithy core, the white albedo inside the thin yellow flavedo; seedy, 10–15 seeds, pale cream, ovoid, pointed, 9–10 mm.
Roots: the sour-orange stock's roots, a short taproot and many woody laterals spreading to the edge of the crown and beyond, most in the top 60 cm, fine fibrous feeder roots yellowish-brown.
