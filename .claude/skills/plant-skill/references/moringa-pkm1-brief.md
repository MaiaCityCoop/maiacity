Moringa oleifera Lam. 'PKM-1' (Moringaceae) — the drumstick tree, murungai, sahjan, of the Horticultural Research Station Periyakulam, Tamil Nadu (1989): the annual moringa grown from seed, the most planted cultivated moringa in India. A fast, soft-wooded tree grown from seed, its leader pinched at about 75 cm in its second month so it branches low into a vase of 5–10 slender ascending branches; flowering from its fourth month (90–100 days), green pods picked from its sixth (160–170 days); cut back to 1 m after each crop and kept at 3–4 m.
Seed: a rounded, faintly three-angled dark brown body 1–1.4 cm across, with three papery straw-coloured wings 1–2.5 cm long down its angles, the seed with its wings about 2.5–3 cm across; 0.27 g. Sown 2.5 cm deep. Germination hypogeal: the root is out at 5 days, the shoot comes up as a hook at 8 days while the seed and its seed leaves stay underground in the coat; the first true leaves, at 13 days, are already twice-pinnate (2 pairs of pinnae of 3–5 small oval leaflets, 6–10 cm long); later leaves grow larger and from about the twelfth leaf thrice-pinnate.
Stem and bark: young shoots pale green to purplish-green, finely hairy; one-year wood grey-green with pale raised lenticels; the trunk of a grown tree short and soon branching, the wood soft and pale yellowish. Old bark pale whitish grey to buff, thick, soft and corky, warty and rough but not deeply fissured, with pale scars where branches were cut; whitish gum oozing from wounds. A bearing PKM-1 in its main crop at 7 months: about 3 m tall, the trunk 9 cm thick near the base, branching from 0.35–0.8 m into 5–10 slender branches rising at 35–55° from upright, the outer ones bending out and drooping under their pods; an open, airy vase-shaped crown 2–2.5 m across.
Leaves: alternate, spirally set, crowded toward the branch tips; large, about 45 cm long (25–60), 2–3 times pinnate, feathery: a slender green petiole 4–15 cm, swollen and jointed at its base, and a rachis carrying 4–8 pairs of opposite pinnae about 5 cm apart, the largest at the base and only single leaflets near the tip; each pinna has 5–11 pinnules of 3–7 leaflets, an odd one at each tip; 75–150 leaflets a leaf. Leaflets of PKM-1 about 2.2 × 1.3 cm, elliptic, the terminal ones obovate and larger, the tip rounded or notched, entire, thin; upper side mid to dark green, underside paler grey-green, matt; small stalked glands at the joints of the rachis. Young leaves pale yellow-green, soft; old ones turn yellow and the leaflets drop. The crown is light and lacy: the sky shows through.
Flowers: in loose, drooping axillary panicles 10–25 cm long near the branch tips, many flowers; each flower 2–2.5 cm across, zygomorphic, sweetly fragrant: 5 narrow reflexed sepals 1–2 cm, cream with yellow streaks; 5 unequal spoon-shaped cream-white petals 1.2–2 cm, the upper one erect and the four others spreading and bent back; 5 fertile stamens with yellow filaments and anthers and 5 shorter staminodes; a slender hairy style. 40–90 flowers a panicle. Buds round, cream-green.
Fruit (the drumstick): a long hanging pod, three-angled with nine ribs, tapering to a point at the tip and beaked, slightly swollen over each seed so it looks faintly beaded, about 70 cm long (61–76) and 2 cm across (girth 6.3 cm), 130 g, hanging straight down on a short stalk 1.5–3 cm, one to three from a panicle; it reaches its full length in about four weeks and is picked green and tender at 45–70 days after flowering, mid green (#5d8f3a); young pods thin and bright light green, all green (no red); mature pods at 100–115 days dry light brown (#a88a62), split along their three angles into three valves, silvery inside, showing the winged seeds. Cut across, a green pod is a rounded triangle with a soft white pith and the soft white seeds in it. 14–20 seeds a pod.
Roots: a swollen, carrot-like tuberous taproot, fleshy, yellowish cream-white, pungent like horseradish, 25–35 cm long and 2 cm thick at 2 months, with a few thin laterals; woody and brown-skinned with age.
