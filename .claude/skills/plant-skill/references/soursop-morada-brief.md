Annona muricata L. 'Morada' — soursop, guanábana, graviola, corossol (Annonaceae): the large-fruited acid type from Colombia (named 'Morada' in Brazil), grafted onto a soursop seedling. A small evergreen tree; its architecture is Troll's model: every shoot grows out and up, its end bends over and lies out level as a branch with its leaves in two flat rows, and a new upright shoot rises from the top of the bend, so the crown is built of arching, overlapping layers. In the orchard it is topped at 0.7 m and grown on 3–4 low scaffold limbs into a rounded, open head kept at about 3.5 m, with NO long bare stem: a SHORT trunk, the crown starting low, like a bush on a short trunk, not a ball on a stick.
Seed and seedling: seed glossy black (drying dark brown), smooth, hard, flattened kidney-shaped, 18 × 11 × 5.5 mm, 0.45 g. Germination epigeal in 2–3 weeks: a pale green hypocotyl hook 4–6 cm pulls up the seed; two thin oblong pale-green seed leaves about 2 × 1 cm open out of the black seed coat, which drops; the first true leaves at about 25 days, small (5 × 2.5 cm), obovate and glossy. The rootstock seedling at a year is 35 cm, its stem 1 cm thick, green-brown.
Trunk and bark: the grafted tree a single short trunk, the graft union 20–25 cm above the soil (a slight step); at its fifth year 3.5 m tall, the trunk 16 cm thick at the base, branching at 0.6–0.8 m into 3–4 limbs at 30–45° from upright, the crown 3.5–4 m wide and dense. Young shoots green-brown with fine rusty-brown hairs; two-year wood brown-grey with small pale raised lenticels; old bark smooth to finely rough, dull grey-brown (#7a7166) with shallow darker fissures and pale lenticel dots, not furrowed into plates.
Leaves: alternate, in two rows on the level branches (each branch with its leaves in one plane like a flat spray), spiral only on upright shoots. Blade obovate-oblong, 12 × 5 cm (8–16 cm), broadest above the middle, tip short-pointed, base wedge-shaped, margin entire, leathery; upper side very glossy dark green (#2b4a1f) with a pale sunken midrib and about 9 pairs of fine lateral veins; underside dull, pale yellow-green (#8fa66a), the midrib raised and pale. Petiole very short, 6 mm, green. Young leaves soft pale yellow-green, the shoot tip rusty-hairy; old leaves pale yellowish green before they fall.
Flower: borne directly on the bark of the trunk and the old limbs and on the twigs (cauliflory), singly or 2–3 together from small woody cushions, on a stout green stalk 1.5–2 cm; it hangs or points out sideways. Bud plump, three-sided, triangular-conical, 3 cm long, green. The open flower 3.5–4 cm across: 3 tiny green triangular sepals 4 mm; 3 thick fleshy outer petals 4 × 3 cm (about 3 mm thick), broadly heart-shaped at the base with a pointed tip, valvate, spreading, yellow-green (#c2c96a) outside, cream-yellow inside; 3 inner petals 3 × 2.5 cm, pale yellow (#efe3a0), concave and overlapping, nearly closed round the centre; inside them a cone of hundreds of small cream stamens and, above, a domed head of many tiny carpels with sticky white stigmas.
Fruit: one big heart-shaped syncarp hanging straight down from a short, stout woody stalk 3 cm long and 2 cm thick, from the trunk and limbs (often low on the trunk). Just after flowering the young fruit is a small green 'brush' of the carpels' styles (2–3 cm); at 30 days an egg 4–5 cm, dark green, spiny; at 90 days 16 × 11 cm; mature at 150 days 25 × 16 cm, 3 kg, broad and heart-shaped, widest in its upper third and rounded to a blunt tip, often a little lopsided. Skin thin and leathery, dark glossy green (#2f4a22) when young, at maturity light dull yellowish green (#8a9a45); covered all over in some 600 soft, fleshy, curved spines 3–8 mm long, one in the middle of each faint polygonal areole about 13 mm across, curving toward the tip; at maturity the spines spread apart and their tips brown. Cut lengthwise: snow-white, juicy, fibrous flesh in segments round a cream central core running the fruit's length, with many glossy black seeds (about 250) set radially in it.
Roots: a taproot when young, then shallow spreading feeder roots, light brown.
