# Manihot esculenta Crantz 'TME 419': facts for a procedural model

Researched 2026-11-05 for a cassava plant grown stage by stage, from the stem cutting to the harvested storage roots.
Built on the template `datasheet.md` and set out like `avocado-fuerte.md`. Cassava is planted from **stem cuttings**
(stakes), not from seed. So the "seed" stage of the model is a 20–25 cm piece of hardwood stem. The true seed
(section 7) matters only for the look of capsules. In the field TME 419 seldom flowers, so it seldom makes them.

**Labels used below**

- **[TME 419]**: measured on TME 419 itself (also written TMEB419, TME-419, TME419, NICASS 20, "419").
- **[SPECIES]**: said of *Manihot esculenta* in general, or of cassava cultivars in general.
- **[EST]**: my estimate for the model, with the reasoning given. It is not a published value.
- **[2nd]**: I read only an abstract, a search snippet, a review or a table that cites the work, not the original.

Each value has a source tag such as S5. The full references are at the end. Units are metres or centimetres,
kilograms, days and months. Ages are **from planting the cutting** (DAP = days after planting, WAP = weeks, MAP =
months).

## The look in 10 lines (for the modeller)

1. **Cutting:** 20–25 cm of brown, mature stem, 2–3 cm thick, with 5–7 nodes. It is set at 45° or upright, with 2/3 of
   its length buried, on a ridge, at 1 m × 1 m. [SPECIES] S17, S18; [TME 419] S6 (25 cm), S7 (upright, 2/3 buried)
2. **Sprouting:** roots from the buried end from ~5 DAP. Shoots from the upper nodes at ~7–15 DAP. The first true
   leaves expand at ~30 DAP. Keep **1–2 shoots**. [SPECIES] S19 [2nd]; shoots kept [EST]
3. **Height:** 0.21 m at 1 month; 1.05 m at 2 months; 1.25 m at 3; 1.37 m at 4; 1.63 m at 5; **1.90 m at 6 months**;
   **~2.5–3 m at harvest** (10–12 months). [TME 419] S5 (Akure, sole crop), S4 [2nd] (~3 m); 8–12 months [EST]
4. **Habit:** **erect, straight, essentially unbranched**. "Non-branching" (S5). 0.33 branches per plant at 6 months
   (S5). "Erect, straight type" (S3). "Late and minimal flowering" (S9, S10, S11). In cassava a fork comes only with
   flowering, so TME 419 is mostly **one tall cane**, or 2–3 canes from the cutting, with a tuft of leaves on top.
   [TME 419]
5. **Stem:** green at the tip, then **brown / light brown**, with prominent leaf scars. Diameter 0.7 cm at 1 month,
   1.7 cm at 2, 1.8 cm at 3, **2.0 cm at 6 months**, ~2.5–3 cm at the base at harvest. [TME 419] S1, S3, S5; harvest
   [EST]
6. **Leaf:** palmate, **5–7 lanceolate lobes** (central lobe ~12–18 × 3–4.5 cm). **Petiole green**, ~15–25 cm long.
   The young leaves are pale yellow-green and the expanded ones bright green, without hairs. [TME 419] S1 (lanceolate,
   green petiole, colours, no hairs), S3 (green petiole); counts and sizes [EST from SPECIES S19, S20]
7. **Canopy:** a thin crown. TME 419 is "taller … with a less dense canopy", and has a lower LAI than IITA-TMS-IBA980581
   (S14 [2nd]). There are ~40 living leaves at 3 months and ~55 at 6 months (S5). A leaf lives 60–120 days (S19
   [2nd]), so the lower stem is bare with leaf scars.
8. **Storage roots:** **~10 per plant** (9.6), **~44 cm long**, **~6 cm thick**, ~0.5 kg each, **4.9 kg per plant**
   (49 t/ha) at harvest. [TME 419] S5. Other trials give 15–36 t/ha. [TME 419] S1, S3, S6, S7, S13, S15
9. **Root colours:** a **brown outer skin**, a **light yellow / cream cortex** (peel) and **white flesh**. The neck is
   **short**. [TME 419] S1, S3
10. **Timing:** storage roots begin to swell **between 30 and 38 DAP** and are clearly tuberous by 60 DAP (S8, at Ibadan).
    The harvest is at **9–13 months** (S6: 10 MAP; S7: 11 > 9 MAP; S15: 13 MAP best). Dry matter is 28–40 %. [TME 419]

## 0. The cultivar in one paragraph

- **What it is.** A CMD-resistant clone from IITA. It was released in Nigeria in **2005 as NICASS 20** (official clone
  name TME419, original name TME-419) [TME 419] S2. Its listed traits are "high dry matter, high CMD resistance, high
  yielding (> 25 t/ha), poundable and high starch" (S2). It is one of the most widely grown improved cassavas of
  Nigeria. It is the usual check variety for gari (S27) and the "Sweet cassava TME 419" of farmers. It has been taken to
  Liberia (S7), Kenya (S13), DR Congo and elsewhere.
- **Its origin is disputed.** Do not model from the genebank accession.
  - The Seed Tracker gives its pedigree as **"83 DTP REP 1 Bulk"** (S1).
  - IITA's *Improved Cassava Variety Handbook* (Dixon et al. 2010, p. 88) calls it "a Togolese landrace" (S16 [2nd]).
  - A breeder wrote that it "was bred by me from TMS-30572" (S16 [2nd]).
  - The IITA genebank accession **TMe-419** resolves to a Togolese landrace, 'Gbazekoute', described differently
    (S16 [2nd]).
- **Its marks.**
  - An erect, straight, tall plant that hardly branches (S3, S5).
  - It flowers late and little (S9, S10, S11).
  - Green petioles and brown stems (S1, S3).
  - Brown-skinned roots with a light yellow/cream cortex and white flesh (S1, S3).
  - It carries the **CMD2** resistance locus (S12 [2nd]).
- **Rules for the model.**
  - One cutting gives one plant.
  - The plant is one clone, so all plants look alike. They differ only by site, crop age and how many shoots came
    from the cutting.
  - Most plants are unbranched canes with a crown of leaves at the top. A minority fork once, late and high.

## Gaps (every row that is an estimate, and how)

- **Lobe number (5–7), central lobe size (14–18 × 3.5–4.5 cm) and petiole length (18–25 cm):** not recorded for TME 419.
  Taken from the middle of the species ranges (S19, S20, S23) for a lanceolate lobe. *Check against close photos of
  TME 419 before building.*
- **Apical leaf colour:** S1 writes "Dark yellow". Fukuda's scale has no yellow (light green, dark green, purplish
  green, purple). I read it as light (yellowish) green. TME 419 is often described by farmers as having pale-green to
  slightly purplish young tips; unverified. *Doubtful.*
- **Stem cortex and epidermis colours:** not recorded; light green cortex, light brown epidermis [EST].
- **Height after 6 months (8, 10, 12 MAP):** extrapolated from S5's curve to the ~3 m of S4 [2nd]. Heights vary a lot
  by site: 1.0–1.25 m (S6), 1.47 m (S26), 1.9 m at 6 MAP (S5), ~3 m (S4).
- **Stem diameter at harvest (2.5–3 cm):** extrapolated from S5 (2.03 cm at 6 MAP) and S6 (1.6–1.7 cm, 5 cm above
  ground). S5 and S6 call the measure "girth", but the values fit a diameter.
- **Branching:** S5 and S3 say non-branching / erect. S4 [2nd] says "branching, profuse". The fork timing and height
  (9–12 MAP at 1.8–2.5 m, for a minority of plants) are inferred from the late, minimal flowering (S9, S10, S11). No
  field record of TME 419's first fork was found. *Doubtful; the most important open point for the silhouette.*
- **Internode length (3.5–4.5 cm):** from the nodes per stake (S17, S18), not measured.
- **Shoots kept (1–2), the angle of the roots and their depth:** estimates.
- **Root mass, length and thickness by month:** a fitted curve through S8 (onset), S5 (harvest) and S7 (9 → 11 MAP
  gain).
- **Root "girth" 6.28 cm (S5):** read as a diameter. If it is a circumference, the roots would be 2 cm thick, which
  cannot hold 0.5 kg. Root mass 0.81 kg (S5) conflicts with 4.92 kg ÷ 9.6 roots = 0.51 kg.
- **Root shape:** conical to conical-cylindrical [EST]; not recorded for TME 419.
- **LAI (2–3), leaf retention and the leaves after 6 months:** estimates from S14 [2nd] and S5.
- **Flower and fruit details:** species-level. TME 419 rarely shows them.
- **Identity:** the genebank accession TMe-419 is a different clone (S16). Never take descriptors from the IITA
  genebank entry "TMe-419".
