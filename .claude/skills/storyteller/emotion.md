# The emotional journey: what the viewer feels, beat by beat

A story is not what happens on screen; it is what happens in the viewer. Before the scenes are written, name the
feelings the viewer should go through, in order — and make each one a contrast to the one before. The tension curve
(`retention.md`) is how strongly they feel; the journey is *what* they feel. Together they are the story's arc line, and
the whole crew reads it: the sound, the grade, the pace and the light follow the feelings.

## Marking it

Every beat of the arc gets three things:
- **the feeling** — one word, or two (*curiosity*, *recognition*, *confinement*, *longing*, *awe*, *relief*);
- **its tension** — 0 (released) … 1 (at its height), as the curve has always had it;
- **its direction** — the tension rising into it (↑, a tension) or released into it (↓, a release).

In the studio it is the Story track of the Script tab: `story_arc` sets every section's points as
`{ t, v, feel }` — where in the section, the tension, the feeling. The track draws the rising stretches in terracotta and
the releases dashed in green, each feeling as a dot on the line with its name (warm feelings gold, cold ones blue, the
open, forward-pulling ones white), and the script lists each part's journey under its title:
*The viewer: curiosity ↑ · recognition ↑ · confinement ↑ · awe ↓*.

## Tension and release are pairs of feelings

A cycle is a feeling that pulls (the tension) answered by one that lets go (the release) — and the release opens the
next pull. Use the pairs:

| Tension (↑) | Release (↓) |
|---|---|
| curiosity, a question | understanding, surprise, delight |
| unease, confinement | relief, freedom, space |
| longing | arrival, belonging, warmth |
| doubt | trust, conviction |
| pressure, urgency | calm, a breath |
| loneliness | togetherness, tenderness |
| disbelief | awe, wonder |

**Contrast makes the feeling.** The release lands only as hard as the tension before it pulled; a feeling next to its
own kind is felt as more of the same. Set each beat against its neighbour: cold against warm, small against vast,
alone against together, still against moving (`retention.md`, "Contrast creates the feeling").

## The shape of the journey

- **Open on curiosity,** never on comfort: the hook's feeling is a question the viewer wants answered
  (`hook-writer`).
- **Make the pain felt before the promise** — recognition (*that's my life*), then unease (`arc.md`, "Lead with the
  pain").
- **Each cycle a little higher** than the last; the deepest cold just before the warmest release (the false summit, the
  dark night: `structure.md`).
- **The peak late,** the warmest feeling at the turn, then a quieter, warm feeling after it.
- **End on an open feeling** — anticipation, a question for tomorrow — warmer than the start (the cliffhanger).
- **Name only feelings the facts can earn.** The vision may be felt fully (awe, longing, hope); Samuel's own feelings
  are only the ones he had (`writing.md`, "Facts only").

## How the crew uses it

- **Sound** (`sound-designer`, `score.md`): spot from the journey — the score changes where the feeling changes; a cold
  feeling gets a sparse, low, unresolved bed, a release a resolved swell; silence before the biggest release.
- **Colour** (`colorist`, `color-story.md`): the colour arc is the journey's warmth — cold feelings desaturated and cool,
  warm ones dense and golden.
- **Pace** (`editor`, `pacing.md`): tension accelerates, release lets the shots breathe; pace by feeling.
- **Light and camera** (`gaffer`, `cinematographer`): confinement in tight frames and a hard ratio; freedom in wide,
  deep, open light; the camera moves the way the viewer should feel.

## The series' signature journey: an epic and intimate Aufbruch (Samuel, 2026-11-03)

Every maiaCITY film carries the same feeling underneath: **a fresh start**. A new way of living, a new civilization,
rebuilt for real (first in the game, then in the world): something inspiring to look forward to, epic and amazing,
and still warm, loving and close, something any person relates to in their own life.

- **Two scales, swapped on every beat.** A close human moment (a child's hand picking fruit, a morning, a family at a
  table, a seed in a palm) against the vast new world it adds up to (a forest you can eat from, a whole city in a
  garden at sunrise). The close moments make the vision touchable; the vision makes the small moments matter.
- **Open and close on the same close moment.** The first shot is intimate and warm; the last returns to it, now
  meaning more, and flows into the first again (the loop).
- **The peak is the vision, felt as awe and belonging at once:** the camera rises from the one close thing to the
  whole new world, and the voice rises with it (the movement's cadence: short lines, repetition, concrete pictures).
- **What a person relates to:** mornings, food, children, time, home, neighbours, health, being part of something.
  Say the epic through them: *a new civilization doesn't start with a parliament; it starts at the breakfast table.*
- **The world as it is today is a gentle recognition, never dread:** the same breakfast now (food from far away, eaten
  in a rush), seen clearly, then the fresh start.
- **The devlog stays human inside it:** we try, we fail, we laugh at ourselves, we fix it, so the viewer roots for us.
- **End on an open door:** "Come build it with us." The viewer leaves as part of the *we*.

## The journey test

- [ ] Every beat has a named feeling and a tension; every part lists its journey
- [ ] Each feeling contrasts with the one before; no two of the same kind in a row
- [ ] Every tension is answered by a release, and every release opens the next tension
- [ ] The deepest cold sits right before the warmest release; the peak is late
- [ ] It opens on curiosity and ends on an open, warmer feeling
- [ ] Epic and intimate swap on every beat; it opens and closes on the same close human moment; the peak is the vision (signature journey)
