An 80 m geodesic dome (Samuel 2026-11-05): its south three quarters a food forest under glass, its north quarter the rooms, four storeys stepping back from the shell as indoor terraces over the forest. Outside, a one-storey limestone arcade round its foot; four round-arched limestone gates (Tore) about 4 m high. Walk in by the south gate.

**The shell.** A class-I icosahedral geodesic of frequency 12 (1,440 triangles, struts 3.1–4.4 m, 751 hubs), its base ring on the ground. To the south, glass in thick white bioceramic frames (0.30 × 0.42 m, rounded) with domed hubs; over the rooms, closed bioceramic panels, white outside with real seams, a warm sand faceted relief inside, and clusters of triangular windows in deep rounded reveals (Geoship's look). A limestone plinth along its whole foot.

**The gates.** East, south and west 4.0 m wide with a semicircular arch to 4.0 m (north 2.6 m, to 3.2 m): coursed limestone quoins, impost mouldings, voussoirs with a proud keystone, a threshold; two heavy oak plank leaves with bronze strap hinges standing open under a glazed oak fanlight; the shell's frames cut exactly round the stone.

**Outside.** A one-storey cloister of 60 dressed limestone piers and semicircular arches (moulded bases and capitals, voussoirs and keystones, ashlar spandrels, a cornice), a sedum roof sloping back to the shell (no terrace on it), oak beams under a lime soffit, a flagstone walk, stone benches, bronze lanterns; a wider bay on each gate. Flagstone paths out to 18 m.

**The forest** (the layout Samuel called perfect). Its floor is play's own biome 'Food forest under glass' (biomes/warm-food-forest@1, by its recipe's hash): the viewer paints the floor mesh with its surfaces and grows its cover (grass, clover, wild strawberry, wood sorrel, ferns, moss, leaf litter) only where the mesh lies. Meandering paths of light grey-white rounded pebble gravel from the three gates to the plaza and round a central clearing with an oak pergola; a brook from a spring in the plaza to a pond by the south gate, river stones on its banks, two arched oak bridges and a ford; and 674 plants of the library by their hashes in seven layers (king coconut, mango, jackfruit, durian, avocado 'Fuerte' v3, sapodilla; orange 'Tarocco' v2, lemon 'Femminello' v2, cacao v2, soursop v2, papaya v2, banana 'Dwarf Cavendish' v2, red banana, fig, pomegranate; coffee, pepper; ginger, comfrey, basil, pineapple; strawberry, nasturtium, clover; passion fruit on the pergola and trellises; oyster and shiitake on logs).

**The north quarter.** Four levels (floors at 0, 4.5, 8.5, 12.5 m; a sky garden on the roof at 16.5 m), each with a curved glass façade of oak mullions stepping back 3.5 m, the slab in front its indoor terrace with a slatted oak rail and planters. Ground floor: a dining hall with a curved kitchen, a library, a workshop, suites. Above: suites with their baths and studios, lounges in the middle bay. Two sculptural spiral stairs and a grand stair of curved flights with white balustrades from the plaza to the sky garden.

**Furnished with 31 things of the library** (each proposed as its own asset of the Things group and placed by its hash: beds, nightstands, mushroom lamps, curved desks and chairs, bouclé sofas and armchairs, travertine tables, poufs, jute rugs, linen curtains, a curved kitchen island and counters, round dining tables with spindle chairs, stools, bookcases, a library table, workbenches, oak benches, pots and troughs, tubs, vanities, toilets, showers, paper lanterns and daybeds); nothing of them is copied in.

**Numbers.** 853k triangles (shell 208k, arcade 159k, building 305k, ground 150k, floor 32k), 33 pictures painted in the box (oak, plank floor, travertine, limestone, lime plaster, bioceramic, sand relief, linen, bouclé, jute, terracotta, pebble gravel, flagstone, river stone, sedum, zellige, bark), 11 lights, 1,236 plants and things marked; it builds in about 3.6–4.5 s (node, measured), within the box's 20 s and its budgets.

**Checked.** Rendered in play's render bench outside and in, with every plant, the biome and every thing drawn (none missing); walked with the viewer's own walker rules (hero.js walkOf): in under the arcade and through the south gate, along the paths and over the bridge, round the clearing, up the plaza's steps and the grand stair into a first-floor suite, up both spiral stairs to the sky garden, and through the hall to the north gate, every step ≤ 0.18 m.

**Known to be short.** A few glazed nooks at the façades' ends are empty; the wedge walls between a stair hall and a turned room are thick at the back of the ground floor; the forest has 21 canopy trees (more would have reshuffled the approved layout); the biome's cover is its first version.
