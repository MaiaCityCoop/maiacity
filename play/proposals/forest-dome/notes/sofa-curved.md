A curved modular sofa in cream bouclé, about 3.1 m along its arc and 0.95 m deep: deep piped seat cushions, a continuous rounded back 0.75 m high, a low oak plinth and two linen cushions.

Made for the Forest dome (buildings group), whose rooms place it by this version's recipe hash: the dome names `things/sofa-curved@1` and draws it once accepted. Real size, built in its parts from the natural-material kit the dome shares (oak, travertine, linen, bouclé, brass, glazed ceramic, paper: each a surface painted in the box, laid in metres). Checked in play's render bench (scripts/render.mjs) from several sides and close up, and its numbers with the box's budget (triangles, pictures, build time).
