A round ribbed paper pendant lantern Ø 0.55 m, its paper on a fine wire spiral and lit from inside, hung on a white cord from a porcelain ceiling rose so its middle is 1.30 m below the ceiling.

Made for the Forest dome (buildings group), whose rooms place it by this version's recipe hash: the dome names `things/lantern-pendant@1` and draws it once accepted. Real size, built in its parts from the natural-material kit the dome shares (oak, travertine, linen, bouclé, brass, glazed ceramic, paper: each a surface painted in the box, laid in metres). Checked in play's render bench (scripts/render.mjs) from several sides and close up, and its numbers with the box's budget (triangles, pictures, build time).
