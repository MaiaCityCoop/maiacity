**Roots stay in their pots** (a fix to v4's terrace, and to v3's rooms).

A plant's roots are part of its build. At full detail a grown lemon or orange reaches 0.9–1.3 m deep and 1.7 m out; a young banana's cord roots run 2.3 m out. In v4 the trees in pots on the terrace showed their roots on the flags (and the deepest through the cloister's soffit), and the strawberries' showed through the troughs. In v3's rooms, the potted bananas and coffee laid their roots across the floors.

**What changes:**
- **Terrace pots:** each of the six by the dies in each quarter is now a great terracotta conca, as a villa's lemons stand in: the library's pot 2.2 times, 1.1 m across. In it is a lemon 'Femminello' in its second year (its stage 3, "Two years": 1.9 m, 2 m across; roots 0.58 m deep and 0.46 m out, inside the pot). Before, they were grown lemons, oranges, a fig and a pomegranate.
- **Troughs:** lavender, rosemary, basil and nasturtiums, all with roots of 0.25 m. Strawberries are gone (theirs spread 0.34 m, wider than a trough). Lavender and rosemary are added to the dome's PLANTS by their hashes (plants/lavender@1, plants/rosemary@1).
- **Rooms:** the pots that held a banana or a coffee now hold a lemon in its first year (stage 2: 1.3 m; roots 0.52 m deep, 0.23 m out) in a pot 1.6 times the library's (0.8 m across). The workshop's pepper stays as it was; its roots fit.
- **`dome.plant` takes `exact`:** a plant's own stage, asked exactly, so a potted tree can be young enough for its pot. This adds two lemon builds (stages 2 and 3) to the dome's builds.

**Unchanged:** the terrace, its balustrade, stairs, pergolas and the rest of its furniture, the arcade, the forest, the paths and the rooms. The build is 1.24 M triangles; it builds in about 3–4 s.

**Checked:**
- The root depth and spread of every potted or troughed plant were measured stage by stage, from production's own sources at full detail.
- Rendered on the bench: the conche and the young lemons by the balustrade, with no roots showing.
- Walked again with the viewer's rules: up the SE stair, along the terrace, down the SW stair. No step is over 0.18 m, and the balustrade, the well and the glass edge stop the walker.
- Every build it names is in play's catalog (61).
