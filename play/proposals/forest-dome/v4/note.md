**The roof terrace (Samuel 2026-11-05: "make the green terrace on the arches a walkable area, a proper terrace like in Sandbox 1, with the new beautiful assets — a community space outside").**

The arcade's sloping sedum roof is now a flat limestone terrace all the way round the dome, at 6.45 m. It runs over the four gates as well, so you can walk the whole ring: about 270 m of promenade, 4.2 m wide.

- **Paving:** real limestone flags in six courses round the ring, each flag its own stone and tint, with mortar joints. A border course runs along the balustrade. A strip of sedum lies along the glass, with a lead flashing against the shell.
- **Balustrade:** it stands on the cornice. A die over each of the 60 piers, about 900 turned limestone balusters between them, and a moulded rail on top at 1.25 m. The walker is stopped at the rail and never reaches the gaps between the balusters.
- **Four stairs up:** one on each diagonal, in the cloister walk under the terrace, curving along the dome's plinth. Each has 36 risers of 17.5 cm on 30 cm treads, in two flights with a landing. The first flight stands on masonry, the second on a sloping slab, and the walk goes on under it. Ashlar walls with copings run on both sides. The stair comes up through a well in the terrace with a 1 m parapet round it, and a threshold flush with the flags at its head.
- **Under the terrace:** the cloister now has a flat lime soffit on oak beams over every pier line, with a limestone wall plate along the shell.
- **Over each gate:** an oak pergola with two round dining tables under it, and passion fruit climbing it from terracotta pots.
- **Furnishing, the same in each quarter:** play's own things and plants, by hash, with a clear lane all round.
  - Along the balustrade: daybeds, armchairs round travertine tables, a curved oak bench.
  - By the glass: round tables looking into the forest, a bench, troughs of basil, strawberries and nasturtiums.
  - In big terracotta pots by the dies: lemons, oranges, a fig and a pomegranate.
  - Bronze lanterns on posts on the rail over every third pier.

**Walk-tested** with the viewer's own walker rules:
- in by the south gate, round the cloister, up the SE stair (no step over 0.18 m) onto the terrace;
- along it over the south gate and down the SW stair;
- the balustrade, the well's walls and the glass's edge each stop the walker without a fall.

The rest is unchanged from v3: forest, paths, rooms, plants. The build is 1.20 M triangles (v3: 0.84 M) and builds in about 4 s.
