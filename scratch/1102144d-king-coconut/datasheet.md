## Height by age

No source measures the king coconut's height at a given age. The table is an estimate [EST], built as follows.

- The trunk shows at about 4 years, as talls' does at 3–4 years (S7, S8, S11).
- It then grows about 0.35 m a year young: between the Sri Lanka dwarf (≈ 0.2 m a year to 17 years) and the Sri Lanka Tall (≈ 0.5 m a year) (S6). CRI calls the king coconut's height growth "medium" (S4).
- It grows about 0.2 m a year from 25 to 40 years, and 0.1 m a year after, as talls slow to 10–15 cm a year from about 40 years (S8, S7).
- That brings it to about 12 m at 60 years, Liyanage's grown height (S1).
- Height to the frond tips is the bare trunk plus the crown's rise, about 3–4.5 m for fronds of 4.3 m held from upright to about 30° out.
